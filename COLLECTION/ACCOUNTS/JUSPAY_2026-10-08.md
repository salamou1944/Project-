## Relevance to Collection

Hyperswitch is materially distinct from Lago/Kill Bill:
- Lago: usage-based billing/metering.
- Kill Bill: subscription/billing/payment gateway abstraction.
- Hyperswitch: payment switching/orchestration, processor connectivity, routing, retries, vaulting, reconciliation and payment operations.

Therefore this source is not a duplicate of the existing Lago/Kill Bill records.
