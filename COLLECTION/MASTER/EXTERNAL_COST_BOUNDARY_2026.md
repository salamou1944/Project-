# EXTERNAL COST BOUNDARY — 2026

Self-hosting can remove recurring software-seat or hosted-platform subscriptions, but cannot automatically eliminate all external costs.

## Costs that may remain
- VPS/server electricity or hosting.
- Domain registration and DNS services.
- Transactional/bulk email delivery and IP reputation operations.
- Payment gateways, card networks, banking APIs and KYC/compliance.
- Social-network APIs and platform-specific rate limits.
- Commercial AI models when local inference is insufficient.
- Proprietary datasets, paid search indexes, proxies or CAPTCHA solving where required.
- SMS/voice carrier charges.
- Object-storage egress and network transfer.
- Hardware/GPU acquisition or rental.

## Collection rule
When a project is marked as a SaaS replacement, record the software subscription eliminated separately from unavoidable external operating costs. Never claim "$0" merely because the repository is open source.
