# God's Eye View — Collection Capture

Date: 2026-10-08
Canonical repository: https://github.com/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view

## What it is
Open-source real-time spatial-intelligence console in the browser: photorealistic 3D Earth with public/live signals including aircraft, ships, satellites, earthquakes, traffic and public cameras, plus hands-free voice control.

## Reusable capabilities
1. 3D geospatial intelligence UI.
2. Multi-layer live/public-signal aggregation.
3. Aircraft/ship/satellite tracking interfaces.
4. Spatial exploration and cockpit-style visualization.
5. Public-camera visualization.
6. Voice-controlled intelligence interface.
7. Modular data-layer architecture.
8. Local-first/keyless startup path for the core experience.

## Strategic value for our collection
VERY HIGH capability value.
This is not merely a visual demo: it is a reusable spatial-intelligence product foundation and an example of combining heterogeneous public signals into one operational interface.

Potential reuse:
- OSINT console
- spatial intelligence layer
- monitoring dashboards
- geospatial agent interfaces
- future products combining Collection data sources with a 3D operational view
