## Why this matters
A combination of RSS + archive + search + crawler + browser + structured storage can turn one-off web research into a persistent intake system. The important design is not merely installing tools; it is preserving source URL, timestamp, content hash/revision, extraction result, verification status, and downstream reuse.

## Important non-equivalence rule
An open-source project is not automatically a drop-in replacement. Record missing features, migration cost, operations burden, cloud-only dependencies, commercial-use restrictions, data portability, security model and external APIs.
