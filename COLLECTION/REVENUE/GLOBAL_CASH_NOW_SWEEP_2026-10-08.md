# GLOBAL CASH-NOW SWEEP — 2026-10-08

## Strategy
Do not filter opportunities by Algeria at discovery time. Search worldwide. Then classify access and payment requirements.

### P0 — Open worldwide / broad remote
- OpenTrain AI: current board states 189 work-from-home roles with no country restriction. Roles include AI evaluation, annotation, transcription, language and coding; verify each role's actual qualification and payout before applying.
- HumanSignal: current onboarding page states remote projects in 100+ countries, weekly payout cadence, and rates shown before work starts. Example rates on the page include AI response evaluation $22–$45/hr and data collection $15–$35/hr; examples are not guaranteed for every country/project.
- Jobpeak: current site advertises worldwide paid AI review work, weekly payment, no CV/interview, no commission, up to $100/hr. Treat as a lead requiring independent due diligence before relying on it.
- Tier1: current board advertises 719 active AI-work roles with published rates; eligibility varies by role, field, country and language.

### P0 — Crypto/stablecoin-compatible opportunity

### P1 — Payment infrastructure / client acquisition
- OneSafe: current site advertises USDC/USDT contractor payouts across 150+ countries. This is more useful as a payment rail for clients/businesses than as a direct earning platform; verify onboarding and jurisdiction before use.

## Discovery directories
- AIPayList currently aggregates 3,068 AI-training jobs from 22 platforms and explicitly warns that advertised top rates are not guaranteed.

## Important distinction
VPN is NOT a universal access solution. If a platform requires actual residence, identity, tax status or KYC in a country, do not falsify those facts. Use a VPN only where the platform's rules permit it. Discovery is worldwide; eligibility is verified per opportunity.

## Payment scoring
For each candidate record:
1. Payment rail
2. Stablecoin supported?
3. RedotPay-compatible wallet/card route?
4. Minimum payout
5. Fees
6. Payout cadence
7. KYC
8. Residence requirement
9. VPN policy
10. AI-assisted-work policy
11. Expected time to first payout
12. Work supply / liquidity
13. Capital required
14. Evidence URL and date

## Priority
P0-A: no-country-restriction + low qualification + weekly/fast payout.
P0-B: USDT/USDC payout + global access.
P1: high-rate expert work matching actual skills.
P1: bounties/creator campaigns with clear payout.
P2: ready businesses and marketplaces.
P3: capability extraction.

## Anti-hype rule
Advertised hourly ceiling, bounty pool, or "up to" amount is not expected income. Do not call an opportunity proven until account acceptance, qualification, task availability, completed work and successful payout are evidenced.
